Read this first: how to use the calculator

1. Check Winter first. The Year 1 Winter section is the same calculation you made in class. It should show Sh4,171 profit and Sh73,546 closing cash. Use “Restore supplied Winter example” if you need to start again.

2. Then enter your real Year 1 ending position. Add your actual cash, machines, loans, and unused tax losses after Year 1 Autumn.

3. Choose real premises and machines. Selecting a premise automatically adds its rent, machine slots, and transport. Selecting a machine uses its real capacity, maintenance, depreciation, and purchase cost.

4. Remember: request is not revenue. Revenue comes only from ice cream actually allocated and sold. Unsold ice cream spoils. Profit and cash are different, so always check both.
